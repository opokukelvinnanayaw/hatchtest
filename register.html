Rules & Guidelines.

5 CHECKPOINTS

Welcome to The HatchPoint Innovations Challenge: Nyansapo Edition. Before unlocking the team application portal, please review and acknowledge the official participation requirements below:

01 / Undergraduate Eligibility (1st – 3rd Year Only)

The challenge is strictly open to 1st, 2nd, and 3rd year undergraduate students (100 to 300 Level) across all academic departments. All team members must be within 1st to 3rd year.

02 / Mandatory WhatsApp Community Join

After completing registration, all team members are required to join the official HatchPoint WhatsApp group for live announcements, mentor connections, and event updates.

Where to join the WhatsApp Group:
  • Post-Registration Screen: A direct "Join WhatsApp Group" button is presented immediately upon submitting your team registration.
  • Floating Icon on Site: Click the green floating WhatsApp widget at the bottom-right corner of any page at any time.

03 / Information & Proposal Integrity

All registration details, member profiles, and submitted concept proposals must be 100% truthful, original, and free of plagiarism. Misrepresentation or false credentials will result in immediate disqualification.

04 / Verified Contact Channels

The phone numbers and email addresses you provide for all team members will serve as the official channel of communication for interviews, updates, and coordinator feedback. Please ensure they are active and accurate.

05 / Team Composition (3 to 5 Members)

Teams must consist of minimum 3 and maximum 5 undergraduate members. Multidisciplinary teams combining engineering, product design, and business strategy are strongly encouraged.
