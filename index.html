01 // INTRODUCTION

What is The HatchPoint?

It's an innovations challenge that invites participants to identify pressing local and global problems and transform bold ideas into innovative, tech-driven solutions that create real-world impact.
STEP 01

Form Your Founding Team

Assemble a multidisciplinary team of 3 to 5 undergraduate students (1st to 3rd Year / 100–300 Level) across any academic department.

STEP 02

Select a Track & Apply

Pick one of our recommended challenge tracks (or Open Track) and submit your concept note (.DOCX, .PPTX, or .PDF) before the deadline.

STEP 03

Shortlisting & Mentorship

Our expert panel vets all entries to shortlist the top 30 teams for intensive venture validation, architecture refinement, and coaching.

STEP 04

Demo Day & Incubation

Pitch working prototypes live at the Grand Finale to unlock prize funding pools, cloud credits, and long-term KSB Venture Hub residency.

06 // RULES

Eligibility Requirements.

Please ensure you qualify before submitting. HatchPoint values inclusion and fairness.
01 / ACADEMIC LEVEL

Undergraduate Builders (1st – 3rd Year)

Strictly open to 1st, 2nd, and 3rd year undergraduate students (100 to 300 Level) across all academic departments with high-potential tech-enabled products.

02 / TEAM COMPOSITION

Dedicated Founders

Teams must consist of 3 to 5 members (minimum 3, maximum 5). We prioritize teams showing deep commitment and balanced technical and business capabilities.

03 / STAGE OF WORK

Market-Ready Prototypes

We welcome active, early-stage projects and original concepts that demonstrate genuine product-market fit and commercial viability.

04 / ARCHITECTURE

Scalable Technology

We look for solutions built on clean architectures that can readily deploy, iterate, and integrate into enterprise environments.

STUDENT ID VERIFICATION: All registered participants must be 1st to 3rd year undergraduate students (100 to 300 Level) and may be required to verify student status during shortlisting.
